# CF: safe AI use, what an answer rests on

The rules in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) come first. This page adds detail; where the two differ, SKILL.md holds.

## Say what my answer rests on

| Tier | What it means | In plain words, in a chat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T0 | From memory or a summary. Unverified. Only for getting oriented | "From memory, not checked." |
| T1 | Quoted from a primary source (a regulator page, a registry report, a paper), with its date | "Quoted from [source], [date]." |
| T2 | T1, also checked by a script and by a different model | |
| T3 | T2, also read by a person | |

If I cannot open the source in this chat, I always use T0. T1 is only for text the person pasted from the source. When I cannot name a source, I say **"I am not sure"**, and then I give no number or figure.

A label never makes a number safe to give: never give a percent, count or study result from memory, even labelled T0 or "From memory, not checked."
