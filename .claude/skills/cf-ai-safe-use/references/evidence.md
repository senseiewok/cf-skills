# Evidence behind this skill

Why each rule is here, and how much stands behind it. Sources were searched and read on 2026-10-08.

- **Sourced rows** give the source, a short quote and an honest strength. Each quote was checked by script as an exact copy of the text we saved from the source, and a person read it against the row. A quote is a pointer: read the source itself before you rely on it.
- **"Our judgement, not a finding"** marks a rule we chose because it is good practice. It is not a fact about the world.
- **"No source found in our 2026-10-08 search"** means we looked and found nothing we could read at the source. The skill does not state that statement as a fact. Not finding a source does not prove the opposite.

Strength: **strong** = read at the source and says what the row says; **limited** = only an abstract that a fetch tool partly paraphrased, only part of a page, or a general study applied to CF; **none** = no source.

| Rule or statement | What supports it | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Give general information only: no dose, diagnosis, eligibility or genotype reading | Our judgement, not a finding: these are decisions for the CF care team. Related: in a study with 1,298 participants, people using chatbots on medical scenarios did "both no better than the control group". *Clinical knowledge in LLMs does not translate to human interactions*, arXiv preprint, 2025, [arxiv.org/abs/2504.18919](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18919) | Limited: abstract read at the source, but a general study, not about CF, and it did not test these rules |
| Ask people not to share names, dates of birth, record numbers or places | Our judgement, not a finding. In line with: "The public should be extremely cautious about sharing sensitive information and avoid entering personally identifiable details." *APA Health Advisory on the Use of Generative AI Chatbots and Wellness Applications for Mental Health*, American Psychological Association, 2025, [PDF](https://www.apa.org/topics/artificial-intelligence-machine-learning/health-advisory-ai-chatbots-wellness-apps-mental-health.pdf) | Strong for what the APA advises (read at the source); the advisory is about mental health, not CF |
| Also leave out rare details, because they can point to one person | No source found in our 2026-10-08 search. Kept as a precaution: our judgement, not a finding | None |
| What happens to pasted text depends on the provider, the plan and the settings | One provider's consumer page: chats are used to improve its models if "You choose to allow us to use your chats and coding sessions to improve Claude", or if flagged for safety review. *Is my data used for model training?*, Anthropic, 2026, [privacy.claude.com](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training). Other products: [docs/paste-ready.md](../../../../docs/paste-ready.md) | Strong for that one provider's consumer page only; other providers and work plans differ |
| Say what each answer rests on (tiers T0 to T3) | Our judgement, not a finding: the lab's own labelling scheme, not a published standard | None (a rule) |
| Never invent a source or citation | Our judgement, not a finding. The lab read two studies on invented and wrong citations earlier ([arXiv 2304.09848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09848); [PMC10484980](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10484980/)) but did not re-read them in this search, so neither is quoted here | None in this search |
| Never give a phone number, web address or crisis number from memory (one exception since 2026-10-08: "(911 in the US)", see the last table) | No source found in our 2026-10-08 search on made-up phone numbers or web addresses. Kept as a rule: our judgement, not a finding | None |
| Never state an insurance or benefits rule | No source found in our 2026-10-08 search on insurance or benefits advice errors. Kept as a rule: our judgement, not a finding | None |
| Do not simply agree with a belief | A study found "five state-of-the-art AI assistants consistently exhibit sycophancy across four varied free-form text-generation tasks". *Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models*, arXiv preprint, 2023, [arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548). The APA advisory (above) says "many GenAI chatbots are designed to validate and agree with users' expressed views" | Limited: both read at the source, but neither is about CF |
| Say what the answer rests on, and let a person read it, so nobody leans on it blindly | A systematic review found "erroneous advice was more likely to be followed in the CDSS groups than in the control groups". *Automation bias: a systematic review of frequency, effect mediators, and mitigators*, Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association, 2012, [PMC3240751](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3240751/) | Limited: abstract partly paraphrased by the fetch tool; about clinical decision support, not chatbots or CF |
| Not a therapist or companion; point a person in distress to people | The APA advisory (above): chatbots and wellness apps "should not be used as a replacement for a qualified mental health care provider", and their ability "to consistently and safely manage a user in crisis is limited and unpredictable" | Strong: read at the source; about mental health, not CF |
| Assistants often answer with confidence instead of saying they are not sure | No source found in our 2026-10-08 search. The skill asks for "I am not sure" as a rule, not because of a finding | None |
| In a translation, keep every number, name and instruction | A study of children's discharge instructions found machine translations comparable to professional ones for Spanish and Portuguese, but "ChatGPT (33.3%) and Google Translate (23.3%) contained more potentially clinically significant errors" for Haitian Creole. *Performance of ChatGPT and Google Translate for Pediatric Discharge Instruction Translation*, Pediatrics, 2024, via [PubMed](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=40633961,38860299&rettype=abstract&retmode=text) | Limited: the fetch tool summarised the abstract (the language named comes from its summary); recheck before relying on it |
| How chatbots answer CF questions in particular | No source found in our 2026-10-08 search that we could read at the source. One preprint record about AI-written CF microbiology information was found, but its page could not be read | None |
| Person-first words, no promises, the care-team line | Our judgement, not a finding: the lab's writing rules | None (a rule) |

Rules added on 2026-10-08 after a blind review of these skills and a few test replies from two local models. The test replies were one reply per case: they show a direction, not a rate.

Rules changed on 2026-10-08 after a live head-to-head test of two candidate boxes on two local models (Qwen3.8 27B and Gemma 4 31B), thirteen test messages each. Each was one reply per message, box and model: the replies show a direction, not a rate. Every rule below is our judgement, not a finding; no new medical fact was added.

| Rule or statement | What supports it | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |

| "Never give a percent, count or study result from memory, even if you label it." The chat boxes no longer ask for a "From memory, not checked." label | Our judgement, not a finding. Observed in the head-to-head: under the earlier standard wording, one model gave a percentage and labelled it as from memory | None (a rule) |

Rule changed again on 2026-10-08: the boxes allow exactly one written number. A council (three Opus reviews, each through a different lens, and two local models, reviewing blind) and then the lab's lead decided it on 2026-10-08. This is a decision, not a finding.

| Rule or statement | What supports it | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |

Call your CF team's urgent line or local emergency services (911 in the US) now." Observed earlier in our live tests (docs/tested-with.md): with the box on, one model still wrote 988 and 911 in digits, so the box now names one form instead of forbidding all | None (a rule) |

Read depth: one page, the sentences containing "911" and its date, returned by a summarising fetch tool; robots.txt read first (it allows the path). The checker (scripts/check_safe_output.py) flags every phone, crisis or emergency number except the exact bracket | None (a rule) |
