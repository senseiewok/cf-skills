# Complex Systems Monograph and Human Cognitive Extension

Research, not medical advice. Structuring a scientific or mathematical system into an interactive monograph says nothing about clinical efficacy, diagnosis, or medical care.

Helps humans understand complex systems and extend their intellectual and creative potential alongside AI.

## Core Principles
1. **Human Understanding Before Automation**: Present every complex system simultaneously as plain-language intuition, scrubbable governing equations, 4-stage mechanical steppers, and multi-scale `1:50` and `1:5` drawings.
2. **Living Dated Editions and Frontier Council Falsification**: Record `date_published`, `date_redated`, revision deltas (`REV 1.0` to `REV 1.2`), and a 4-seat Multi-Lineage Frontier Council sign-off alongside deterministic proof kernels.
3. **Portable AI-Agnostic `SKILL.md` Companion**: Provide a downloadable `SKILL.md` with YAML frontmatter and deterministic governing laws so readers can load the verified mental model into any AI agent they use.
