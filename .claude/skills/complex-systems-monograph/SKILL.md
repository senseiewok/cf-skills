# Complex Systems Monograph & Human Cognitive Extension (`complex-systems-monograph`)

Helps humans understand complex systems and extend their intellectual and creative potential in the era of frontier AI.

## Core Principles
1. **Human Understanding Before Automation**: Every complex system is presented simultaneously as plain-language intuition, scrubbable governing equations (Bret Victor), 4-stage mechanical steppers (Bartosz Ciechanowski), and multi-scale `1:50` / `1:5` drawings (*DETAIL*).
2. **Living Dated Editions & Frontier Council Falsification**: Every monograph records `date_published`, `date_redated`, revision deltas (`REV 1.0 -> REV 1.2`), and a 4-seat Multi-Lineage Frontier Council sign-off (including Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-OSS 120B, and deterministic proof kernels).
3. **Portable AI-Agnostic `SKILL.md` Companion**: Every publication provides a downloadable `SKILL.md` with YAML frontmatter (`name`, `description`, `license`, `compatibility`) and deterministic equations so readers can load the verified mental model into any AI agent they use.
