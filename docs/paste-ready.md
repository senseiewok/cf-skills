# Where to paste a paste-ready box

We read these help pages on 8 October 2026. Apps change often, so check the app's own help page.

Five CF skills in this repository have a `paste-ready.md` file: [`cf-ai-safe-use`](../.claude/skills/cf-ai-safe-use/references/paste-ready.md), [`cf-plain-language-rewrite`](../.claude/skills/cf-plain-language-rewrite/references/paste-ready.md), [`cf-visit-prep`](../.claude/skills/cf-visit-prep/references/paste-ready.md), [`cf-answer-check`](../.claude/skills/cf-answer-check/references/paste-ready.md) and [`cf-ai-tool-review`](../.claude/skills/cf-ai-tool-review/references/paste-ready.md). Each file has two boxes (blocks) of plain text: SHORT and STANDARD. You copy one box into an AI app. There is nothing to install.

**Use the standard box where your tool accepts it; if your tool says the text is too long, use the short box.**

> **Before you paste**
>
> - If you are on a care team, use the AI tool your organisation approves. If the question is about your own or your family's health, think about who can see your chats. A work account belongs to your workplace, so check its policy, or use a personal account.
> - Do not put patient information in: no names, dates of birth, record numbers, places or rare details.
> - Settings and plans differ, and these pages change. Check the official page for your product and plan.

## The simplest way, in any product

1. Start a new chat.
2. Paste the box. Below it, in the same message, write your question. Then send.
3. If the assistant answers something else, or says the text is too long, start a new chat and use the short box.

Start a new chat for each task. This needs no settings, only a chat box.

The rest of this page says where to paste in four common products. It uses only what their makers' help pages said when we read them. Research, not medical advice.

| Feature | Steps | Limit | Official page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instructions for Claude (your account) | Click your initials in the lower left corner, select "Settings", then under "Instructions for Claude" describe your instructions | No limit stated on the page we read | [Understanding Claude's personalization features](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10185728-understanding-claude-s-personalization-features) |

The personalization page says "Projects are available to all users, including those with free Claude accounts". But our tool's summary of a comparison on the same page said project instructions are for paid plans only. We could not settle this, so check in your account. For organization instructions the page says "When both are set, organization instructions take precedence."

**Data use, read 2026-10-08.** Consumer plans: "We will use your chats and coding sessions (including to improve our models) if: 1. You choose to allow us..." ([Is my data used for model training?](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training), updated 16 March 2026). Workplace plans: that page covers consumer products. It points to a separate article for commercial products, which we did not read.

## Which box fits where

Block sizes in this repository, measured on 2026-10-08: SHORT 1,066 to 1,199 characters (a test keeps each at most 1,200); STANDARD 2,901 to 3,252 (a test keeps each at most 3,300). Sizes count the text inside a box, not the fence lines around it. Each skill's file shows its own sizes above each box.

| Where | Limit we found | SHORT | STANDARD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft Agent Builder, Instructions field | 8,000 characters | Fits | Fits |
| Claude organization instructions | 3,000 characters | Fits | Fits for `cf-ai-safe-use`. Over 3,000 characters, so it does not fit, for `cf-ai-tool-review`, `cf-answer-check`, `cf-plain-language-rewrite` and `cf-visit-prep`: use their short box there |
| Claude account or project instructions; Gemini Gems; Instructions for Gemini | No limit stated on the page we read | Test a paste | Test a paste |
| ChatGPT | Pages not read | Use this one | Read the maker's page first |
| The first message of a new chat | No limit stated on the pages we read | Test a paste | Test a paste |

**Test a paste** means: paste the box and save. Then open it again and check that the last line is still there. If the product refuses the text, or the end is missing, use a shorter box. The pages we read do not say how each product counts characters.
